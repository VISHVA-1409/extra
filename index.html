Exam & Grading Information

Assignment Score: 25% of average of best 8 assignments out of 12.

Exam Score: 75% of the proctored certification exam score out of 100.

Final Score: Average assignment score + Exam score

Exam Pattern: 100 × 1 = 100 Marks or 50 × 2 = 100 Marks

Important: Please bring your Hall Ticket and a valid ID card to the exam hall.
